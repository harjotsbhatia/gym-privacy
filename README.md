# DopaLift Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

## Summary

DopaLift has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The developer doesn't collect your data and can't see your workouts. It isn't sold, or used for advertising.

## Your workouts

Workouts, plans, custom exercises and settings are stored on your device. They may be included in your device backups, depending on your backup settings.

## Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, DopaLift asks to read active energy and workouts, which it uses for your Home summary.

If you turn on sync, DopaLift saves completed workouts to Apple Health. You can change these permissions at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app with a new date.
