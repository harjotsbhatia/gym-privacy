Gym Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Summary

Gym has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The developer doesn't collect your data and can't see your workouts. It isn't sold, or used for advertising.

Your workouts

Workouts, plans, custom exercises and settings are stored on your device. They may be included in your device backups, depending on your backup settings.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, Gym asks to read active energy, heart rate, steps and workouts. It uses active energy and workouts for your Home summary, and steps for step challenges. It doesn't currently use heart rate.

If you turn on sync, Gym saves completed workouts to Apple Health. You can change these permissions at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access.

Friend Challenges

Challenges are optional and use Apple's iCloud. When you create or join one, its title, dates and metric, your display name and your running total are stored in iCloud and shared with the other participant.

That total is your workout count, weight lifted, streak or, for a step challenge, step count from Apple Health. Your individual workouts aren't shared. iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't read it.

Exercise animations

Exercise demo animations are loaded from cdn.fitsw.com, a third-party service. As with any web request, that service receives your IP address and basic request details when an animation loads. Your workout data isn't sent.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. To remove challenges you created, delete Gym's data from your iCloud storage in your device settings. Progress you shared in a challenge someone else created is stored in their iCloud, so contact the developer if you need help with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and in the app with a new date.
